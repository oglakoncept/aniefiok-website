My Working Frameworks

The Systems Behind the Practice

Exploring the systems, tools, and thinking approaches behind effective solutions.

Step 01

Listen & diagnose the problem

Starting with stakeholder conversations and data review to understand the real gap before designing a solution.

Step 02

Design the framework

Translating that diagnosis into a practical system, tool, or process built for the context it will operate in.

Step 03

Build with the Team for Sustainability

Co-creating with the people who will own it, so the system outlives the consultancy.

Step 04

Institutionalize & handover the product

Embedding the tool into existing workflows and policy so it becomes standard practice, not a one-off deliverable.

Step 05

Track what’s working and adapt

Monitoring uptake and outcomes, then refining the system as evidence comes in.
