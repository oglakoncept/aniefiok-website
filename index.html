Case Studies

Selected Work in Depth

Three engagements, from challenge through approach to result.

Health System Vulnerability Assessment

Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare · Nigeria · May 2026
Challenge

Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Health needed to understand the health system's vulnerability to external geopolitical and economic shocks in order to reprioritize national investment.

Approach

Led a rapid assessment across 60 federal and state institutions and 2,121 data points spanning seven health system domains, applying a risk-scoring framework to rank critical vulnerabilities.

Result

Findings informed NHSRII reprioritization, with key results presented by the Honourable Minister of Health and Social Welfare at the Geneva Conference to inform global health resilience discussions.

KM Strategy Adoption and Institutionalization

LAFIYA (UK-Nigeria Partnership for a Healthier Future) / Palladium · Nigeria · Oct 2025–Mar 2026
Challenge

Nigeria's health sector lacked an institutionalized system for capturing and using knowledge, risking the loss of programme learning once the consultancy ended.

Approach

Established a national KM system aligned with the Health Sector KM strategy and the ISO 30401 international standard, built governance structures and a national Community of Practice, and developed a three-tier training curriculum with SOPs and guidelines.

Result

Left the Ministry able to independently manage its KM functions beyond the consultancy, with a KM workplan developed and evidence use embedded into routine planning and decision-making.

Disability Inclusion in DRR Baseline

CBM & Sustainable Family Healthcare Foundation (SFHF) · Kogi State, Nigeria · Mar–Apr 2026
Challenge

A disability inclusion and disaster risk reduction programme in Kogi State needed a measurement foundation before rollout, along with a clear picture of the specific gaps it should target.

Approach

Used a stratified, multi-instrument data collection design to capture a representative cross-section of disability status and geographic experience.

Result

Delivered quantified, indicator-level benchmarks and identified specific gaps in policy, practice, and community awareness, directly shaping implementation priorities from day one.
